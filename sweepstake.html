STEP 2 FIFA World Cup 2026™ Format
📋 Group Stage
• 48 teams in 12 groups of 4
• Each team plays 3 matches (vs every team in group)
• Win = 3 points · Draw = 1 point · Loss = 0 points
• Top 2 from each group advance
• Best 8 third-placed teams also advance
• 32 teams total progress to knockouts
🗓️ Group Stage Tiebreakers
If teams are level on points, FIFA apply:
1. Goal difference (all group matches)
2. Goals scored (all group matches)
3. Head-to-head results between tied teams
4. Fair play (fewer cards)
5. Drawing of lots (last resort)
⚡ Knockout Stage
From 28 June 2026:
• Round of 32 → Round of 16
• Quarter-finals → Semi-finals
• Third-place play-off (18 Jul)
• Final — 19 July 2026 · MetLife Stadium, NJ

Drawn after 90 min → 30 min extra time
Still drawn → Penalty shootout

STEP 4 Sweepstake Rules
1️⃣
One Entry Per Person
Each Assurance Fanatics member may enter once and pick one team. You cannot swap, transfer, or share your team entry with anyone else.
⏰
Registration Deadline
All picks must be submitted before the opening match on 11 June 2026. Once the first whistle blows, registration closes permanently.
🎯
First Come First Served
Teams are allocated in the order players register. Popular teams like Brazil, England, and Argentina will go fast — get in early to maximise your choice!
🤝
Tie-Breaking
If two players finish on equal tokens, the tiebreaker is: (1) further team progress, (2) most group stage wins, (3) earliest registration time.
📲
Results Entry
Tokens are updated manually after each match using the Admin token button. The leaderboard refreshes in real time for all players.
